I write to make sense of the world.

Not to teach, not to perform expertise — but to think out loud. Writing forces a kind of rigour that thinking alone doesn't. When an idea only lives in your head, it can stay comfortably vague. The moment you try to put it into words, it either sharpens or falls apart. I find both outcomes useful.

This page is a collection of things I've been thinking about: business, investing, people, books, cities, running, and whatever else refuses to stay quiet in my head. The views here will shift over time, and I think that's a feature, not a bug.

## What to expect

No newsletter cadence. No content strategy. Just writing when something feels worth saying.

Some posts will be long-form. Others will be short notes. Some will age well; others won't. I'm okay with all of that.

## A note on the title

"My little corner on the internet" — because that's all this is. Not a platform, not a brand. A corner.

I hope you find something useful here, or at the very least, something that makes you think.

— Yash
