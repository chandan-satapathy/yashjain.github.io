My name is Yash Jain. Professionally, I work as a consultant, and my hobbies include writing, watching films, running, and travelling.

I was born and brought up in Delhi. I pursued my bachelor's from SSCBS (DU) and did my MBA from IIM Ahmedabad. I have worked/interned at firms such as BCG, HealthQuad (VC), General Catalyst, and most recently Roland Berger.

I write to make sense of the world and this page reflects my beliefs (ever-changing) about business, world, and life in general.
